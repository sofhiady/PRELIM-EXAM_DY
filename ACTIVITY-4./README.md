## ✦ What I Learned

- I learned that digital tools can be used not only to create outputs but also to organize ideas, communicate information, and present work professionally.
- I developed a better understanding of how presentation design, creativity, organization, and digital documentation contribute to the quality of a project.
- One challenge in completing academic digital projects is organizing information and ensuring that the final output is both visually appealing and understandable. I learned to approach this by planning my content, organizing my materials, and reviewing my work before submission.
- I realized that the effective use of technology requires both creativity and attention to detail, especially when presenting work to an audience.
- In the future, I can apply these skills to academic presentations, digital portfolios, collaborative projects, documentation, and professional work.
