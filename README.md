### A LOOK BACK AT MY PREVIOUS WORK

*Some of the ideas, designs, and projects that shaped my creative journey.*

</div>

---

## 01 — KAPENG UMAGA

**Coffee • Branding • Web Design**

A coffee-themed website concept designed around the idea of slow mornings, comfort, and good coffee.

The design explored visual storytelling, menu presentation, typography, color combinations, and a welcoming digital experience.

**Design direction:**
- Warm and welcoming visual identity
- Coffee-inspired elements
- Menu layout and presentation
- Website structure and visual hierarchy

---

## 02 — MAISON MIEL

**Café & Patisserie • Web Design**

A café and patisserie-inspired website concept focused on iced coffee, warm aesthetics, and elegant digital presentation.

The project explored how typography, spacing, colors, imagery, and layout can create a cohesive brand experience.

**Design direction:**
- Elegant café-inspired design
- Minimal and editorial layout
- Product-focused visual presentation
- Consistent branding and typography

---

## 03 — LIKHA LEATHER WORKS

**Branding • E-Commerce • Creative Design**

A brand concept centered around personalized leather paddle covers and customization.

The project explored branding, storytelling, product presentation, and the development of an identity that communicates craftsmanship and personalization.

**Design direction:**
- Minimalist branding
- Earth-toned color palette
- Product storytelling
- Personalized product concept

---

## 04 — BOOTH 7: FREE COFFEE!

**Poster Design • Promotional Material • Visual Communication**

A promotional poster concept designed to communicate a coffee giveaway through bold typography, coffee-inspired illustrations, and an organized visual hierarchy.

The project explored how visual elements can communicate information quickly while creating an engaging promotional design.

**Design direction:**
- Coffee-themed graphics
- Bold typography
- Promotional layout
- Visual hierarchy and readability

---

## 05 — GOOGLE SLIDES

**Presentation Design • Visual Storytelling**

A presentation project exploring the use of layouts, typography, visual elements, and structured content to communicate information.

The project helped develop my ability to organize information and create presentation materials that are visually engaging and easy to understand.

**Design direction:**
- Presentation layout
- Typography
- Visual organization
- Content hierarchy

---

## 06 — SOFHIA WITH THE UNNECESSARY "H"

**Personal Branding • Identity Design**

A personal identity concept that explores branding, typography, simplicity, and personality.

The project demonstrates how a name, visual identity, and design elements can come together to create a memorable personal brand.

**Design direction:**
- Minimalist identity
- Blue color palette
- Typography-centered design
- Personal branding

---

## 07 — EVERY SECOND MATTERS

**Infographic • Healthcare • Visual Communication**

An infographic concept about long hospital waiting times and their impact on patient experience and healthcare delivery.

The project explored how information, statistics, design, and visual communication can be used to present a healthcare-related issue clearly.

**Design direction:**
- Informative infographic
- Healthcare-related visual elements
- Data presentation
- Problem-and-solution communication
