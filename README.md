# ✦ MY REFLECTION

GE 4120 taught me that technology is not simply about using digital applications, but about using them with creativity, purpose, and critical thinking. Throughout the activities, I explored how design, communication, AI, and digital platforms can help transform ideas into meaningful outputs.

Each activity allowed me to approach a different challenge, whether it was designing a promotional flyer, developing a personal brand, presenting healthcare information, exploring AI-generated websites, or evaluating digital documents. I gained a better appreciation for how creativity and technology work together.

As a nursing student, I believe these skills can also be valuable beyond this course. Technology plays an increasingly important role in healthcare, education, communication, and professional development. Learning to use digital tools effectively can help me become more adaptable, creative, and prepared for future opportunities.

This portfolio represents my outputs, experiences, and growth throughout GE 4120 — and a reminder that learning continues beyond the classroom.
