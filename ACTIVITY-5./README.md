# ☕ Kapeng Umaga vs. Maison Miel

> *The quality of the prompt shapes the quality of the output.*

## 📌 Activity Description

This activity involved comparing two cafe website concepts, Kapeng Umaga and Maison Miel, to examine how prompt specificity affects the design and development of AI-generated websites. Through the comparison, the activity explored how details such as branding, content, website structure, visual elements, and specific instructions can influence the final result.

## ✦ What I Learned

- I learned that the quality and specificity of a prompt can significantly influence the structure, appearance, and content of an AI-generated website.
- I learned that providing clear instructions about a website's purpose, target audience, branding, products, layout, and design preferences can help produce a more organized and customized result.
- One challenge was comparing the outputs fairly and identifying differences beyond their appearance. I addressed this by considering factors such as content, organization, design consistency, and how well each website followed the intended instructions.
- I realized that AI tools can help accelerate website creation, but the user still needs to evaluate the output and provide thoughtful instructions to achieve the desired result.
- In the future, I can improve my prompt-writing skills by giving more specific instructions, refining prompts through multiple iterations, and evaluating the generated website before considering it complete.
