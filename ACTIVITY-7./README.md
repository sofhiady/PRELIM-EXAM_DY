# 🤖 Comparative Resume Generation Using Multiple LLMs

> *AI can assist. Critical thinking decides.*

## 📌 Activity Description

This activity involved using two large language models, ChatGPT and Claude, to generate resumes based on the same personal information and requirements. The resulting documents were compared based on their layout, formatting, organization, readability, and use of page space. The activity aimed to evaluate how different AI tools interpret instructions and generate professional documents.

## ✦ What I Learned

- I learned that different AI tools can generate different document structures and layouts even when provided with similar information and instructions.
- I learned how to evaluate AI-generated outputs by considering readability, formatting, organization, consistency, and the effective use of space.
- One challenge was determining which resume was more effective rather than simply choosing the one that looked more attractive. I addressed this by comparing the layouts, organization, and how well the information was presented.
- I realized that AI-generated content should not automatically be accepted as the final output. It requires human review, judgment, editing, and verification to ensure that the result is appropriate and accurate.
- In the future, I can apply this skill when using AI for document creation, professional writing, research assistance, and other tasks where comparing and refining generated outputs is important.

## 💭 My Evaluation

I preferred the resume generated by Claude because of its single-column arrangement, spacing, organization, and effective use of page space. I found its layout more visually organized and easier to follow.

This comparison helped me recognize that AI-generated outputs may differ in quality and presentation, and that choosing the most appropriate output requires evaluation rather than relying solely on the tool.
