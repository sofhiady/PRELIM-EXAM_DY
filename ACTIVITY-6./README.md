# 🧵 Likha Leather Works

> *Made personal. Made with purpose.*

## 📌 Activity Description

This activity focused on developing a website concept for Likha Leather Works, a customizable leather pickleball paddle-cover business. The project involved conceptualizing an online presence that showcases the brand, its products, and personalization options while providing potential customers with an organized and engaging digital experience. It combines creativity, branding, website planning, and entrepreneurship.

## ✦ What I Learned

- I learned how a website can serve as a digital platform for presenting a business, showcasing products, communicating brand identity, and helping customers understand what a business offers.
- I learned the importance of considering the user experience when planning a website, particularly when presenting product options, personalization features, and information about the business.
- One challenge was translating a business concept into a clear and organized website structure. I addressed this by identifying the business's purpose, its target customers, its products, and the features that would make the website useful.
- I realized that a successful website should combine visual appeal, functionality, clear information, and a consistent brand identity instead of focusing solely on appearance.
- In the future, I can improve the concept by exploring additional website features, refining the user experience, developing a more detailed product catalog, and considering customer feedback.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Establish a consistent brand identity for Likha Leather Works.
- Showcase customizable leather pickleball paddle covers.
- Present product information in an organized and visually appealing way.
- Explore personalization features for potential customers.
- Create a convenient digital experience for browsing products.
- Use website design to support a business concept.
